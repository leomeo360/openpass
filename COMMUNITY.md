# OpenPass Community Edition

OpenPass Community Edition is the self-hosted, open-source core of OpenPass.

## Included

- a single logical workspace per deployment
- projects, scoped virtual keys, model permissions, quotas, and request logs
- encrypted provider credentials and provider failover
- fixed project roles for administrators and read-only members
- password, OAuth, or Cloudflare Access authentication
- Cloudflare Workers, D1, and Durable Objects support

## Not included

The commercial edition may add multi-workspace administration, organization switching, departments or teams, custom roles, SSO/SCIM, approval workflows, cost centers, and advanced audit governance.

Community contributions should remain deployable without commercial services and must not introduce production credentials, private deployment data, or dependencies on the private repository.
